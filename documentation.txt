APULA (Atmospheric Prediction & Unified Low-gravity Analysis)
NASA Space Apps Project (Project SIKLAB / ALAB)

Overview

APULA is a Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS engineering dashboard for evaluating spacecraft flammability, microgravity and partial-gravity combustion physics, and empirical NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) flight test telemetry in a unified interface. The repository boots pre-populated with all 8 empirical NASA PSI campaigns (654 normalized test rows) from datum/masterPsiData.ts so users can inspect physics regimes, flame morphology, flammability phase boundaries, and closest flight tests immediately without uploading files.

1. datum/masterPsiData.ts

Stores MASTER_PSI_CSV as a pre-bundled multi-investigation CSV export covering 654 flight test runs across 8 NASA PSI campaigns: FLEX (274), BASS-II (129), BASS (122), SAME-R (109), FLEX-2 (8), SAME (8), SAFFIRE-3 (2), and DAFT (2). Separating the dataset into datum/masterPsiData.ts keeps telemetry data decoupled from both the TypeScript physics engine and the React UI while enabling instant zero-upload boot.

2. src/lib/combustionPhysicsEngine.ts

Separates empirical CSV ingestion, combustion physics modeling, SVG/Canvas visualization generators, and telemetry matching from the UI layer.

Data Ingestion and Normalization Pipeline:
- loadMasterPsiDataset(rawMasterData): Accepts either the raw CSV string from datum/masterPsiData.ts or an array/container of row objects and normalizes every entry into NormalizedRun[]. Preserves exact experimentId labels across FLEX, FLEX-2, BASS-II, BASS, SAFFIRE-3, SAME-R, SAME, and DAFT without misclassifying FLEX-2xx test numbers as FLEX-2.
- parseAndNormalizePsiCsv: Handles both raw NASA BASS-II tables (defaulting experimentId to "BASS-II" and missing pressure to 14.7 psi ISS MSG ambient) and the multi-investigation normalized schema while tolerating missing optional cells and extra study columns. Automatically converts pressures above 25 from kPa to psi (* 0.145038), pressures between 0 and 3.5 atm to psi (* 14.6959), fractional O2 values (0 to 1) to percentages, and strings like "Microgravity (ISS)" to 0g.
- extractMaterialGeometry: Parses unstructured fuel descriptions (including acrylic BASS-II rod, micron film, and spherical section samples) into structured baseMaterial, geometryType, thicknessMm, and widthCm fields.
- extractAirflowTrajectory: Extracts non-negative stepped MSG fan velocity sequences (initial, final, minimum, and array steps in cm/s) and classifies extinction mechanisms such as diffusive quenching, convective blowoff, oxygen depletion, or sooting.

Combustion Physics Math (simulateCombustionPhysics):
- Froude-based effective flow: Computes natural buoyant velocity as 22 * sqrt(gravityG) cm/s and ACME E-FIELD ion wind velocity as 0.008 * electricFieldVoltsCm cm/s, combining natural draft in quadrature with forced cabin ventilation: sqrt(airflowCmS^2 + (buoyant + ionWind)^2). Equivalent gravity is backed out from total natural draft as ((buoyant + ionWind) / 22)^2.
- U-shaped Limiting Oxygen Index (LOI): Adds a low-flow diffusive quenching penalty below 2 cm/s (accumulation of combustion products and radiative heat loss) and a high-flow convective blowoff penalty above 12 cm/s. Applies pressure scaling (pressurePsi / 14.7)^(-0.25) so reduced exploration atmospheres increase diffusivity and lower LOI, alongside thickness correction 0.22 * log2(thicknessMm / 0.35) and CO2 inerting (0.32 * co2DilutionPct).
- Regime classification: Classifies operating conditions into COOL_FLAME_CFI_G, CRITICAL_ACCELERATED_BURN, SPHERICAL_DIFFUSION_FLAME, ELONGATED_MICROGRAVITY_FLAME, NO_IGNITION_QUENCHED, or NO_IGNITION_BLOWOFF, and estimates flame spread rate, adiabatic flame temperature, CO/CO2 yields, and smoke/soot index.

Visualization and Telemetry Matching:
- generateFlameRenderGeometry: Maps simulation results to core flame length, width, aspect ratio, inside-out burner state, soot tail opacity, and buoyant flicker frequency in Hz.
- generateAdvancedFlameField: Computes a deterministic 640x380 Microgravity Wind-Tunnel & Schlieren Diagnostics coordinate field anchored at (170, 190). Outputs 4 nested cubic Bezier zones (schlierenHaloPath, reactionSheetPath, sootMantlePath, pyrolysisCorePath) with gradient stops, plume tilt angle and lift standoff, 6 wake vortex nodes, 11 obstacle-deflected streamlines, a radiative FOV radiometer cone, a 5-point thermocouple rake, and a SAME/DAFT aerosol laser obscuration sensor overlay.
- generateFlammabilityEnvelopeData and generateFlammabilityPhaseMatrix: Builds both the 0g/0.16g/0.38g/1g LOI envelope curves and high-resolution phase matrices: a 23x19 pressure-by-O2 risk heatmap (4.0 to 15.0 psi vs 10 to 100% O2), a 26x19 airflow-by-O2 U-curve risk heatmap (0 to 50 cm/s vs 10 to 100% O2) tagged by quenching/super-flammable/blowoff zones, three iso-spread contours (1, 5, and 15 mm/s), the partial-gravity bounding polygon between 0g and 1g baselines, a 12-waypoint EVA pre-breathe decompression risk tradeoff vector (from ISS 14.7 psi / 21% O2 through Lunar 10.2 psi / 34% O2 to EVA suit 4.3 psi / 100% O2), and projected empirical telemetry pins across all loaded flight tests.
- findClosestEmpiricalRuns: Computes normalized L1 distance across oxygen percentage, effective airflow, pressure, gravity, and fuel family to return the closest historical NASA flight tests.

3. src/data/psiCatalog.ts

Imports MASTER_PSI_CSV directly from datum/masterPsiData.ts and initializes LOADED_MASTER_PSI_RUNS via loadMasterPsiDataset. Defines all 13 NASA PSI investigations across the 3 Fire Safety Pillars (Prevention, Detection, Suppression) and 3 Data Provenance Tiers:
- EMPIRICAL_CSV_LOADED: Investigations backed directly by empirical flight rows loaded from datum/masterPsiData.ts (BASS-II, BASS, SAFFIRE-3, FLEX, FLEX-2, SAME, SAME-R, DAFT).
- PHYSICS_AND_METADATA_MODEL: Gaseous CIR studies modeled by the analytical engine (ACME-BRE, ACME-CFI-G, ACME-E-FIELD).
- FUTURE_LUNAR_FLIGHT: Upcoming Lunar surface studies without PSI telemetry yet (LUCI, FM2), bounded between empirical 0g and 1g flammability curves.
Also provides getRunCampaignId and rankPsiInvestigations to score study relevance for the active habitat state and display live loaded row counts per campaign.

4. src/app/page.tsx

Client dashboard organized into 7 synchronized sections driven by habitat presets (ISS, Lunar Base, Mars Habitat, EVA Suit, Earth), continuous parameter sliders, and instant masterfile boot with optional CSV append/replace import:
- Section 0: Executive Mission Banner explaining the 3 Fire Safety Pillars, analytical vs. empirical provenance tiers, and pre-loaded telemetry status.
- Section 1: Habitat & Experimental Control Panel plus masterfile/CSV ingestion controls.
- Section 2: Real-time CombustionSimulationResult metrics, analytical diagnostics, and Section 2C (src/components/ThreeFlameChamber.tsx), an interactive 60 FPS 3D Three.js Microgravity Wind-Tunnel & Flame Morphology Chamber featuring 3 concentric additive-blended morphing sphere-to-teardrop flame meshes, 180 GPU wind-tunnel streamline and soot wake particles, orbit/zoom camera controls, and a toggleable sensor probe HUD.
- Section 3: Phase-Space Flammability Matrices (src/components/FlammabilityPhaseCharts.tsx) with a Y-axis scale toggle (defaulting to the 12% to 38% O2 boundary zoom mode with an upper scale break callout track for the 100% O2 EVA Suit pin, plus full 0% to 100% O2 scale), smooth continuous thermal boundary gradients, leader-line habitat callouts with zero label collisions, binned empirical telemetry cluster pins, and aerodynamic regime shading across the Ventilation U-Curve.
- Section 4: Dynamic PSI Investigation Ranker filterable by pillar and provenance tier with live campaign row counts.
- Section 5: Cross-Investigation Variable Comparator contrasting fixed and swept parameters across any two selected NASA studies.
- Section 6: Closest Empirical NASA PSI Flight Test Matcher displaying the top 5 matching runs filtered across all campaigns or individual missions.
